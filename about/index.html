Mathilde Schibler engages with communication and digital technology through socially engaged design. She examines how interactivity reshapes our perceptions, builds connection, and questions our relationship to others.

After a Bachelor's in Visual Communication and a Master's in Media Design from HEAD–Geneva, she now develops personal and commissioned projects addressing social, inclusive, and climate-related issues.

She conceives of design as interaction: through an ecosystem of collaborators, and through the publics themselves — not to design for, but to truly design with.
